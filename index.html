Questions

Does it attack or stress the sites I scan?

No. It sends ordinary requests, the same a browser sends when you open a page. You can set the delay between requests and how many pages are checked at once.

Is there really no URL limit in Pro?

Yes. Pro scans follow every link they find unless you set a maximum yourself. Very large sites take longer and use more memory, so keep Chrome open until the scan finishes.

How do I activate Pro?

After checkout you get a license key by email. Open Web Scanner, choose Get Pro, then I have a key, and paste it. To move it to another browser, remove it in the license panel first.

Which browsers does it run in?

Chrome 116 or newer.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, within 14 days of purchase. Reply to your receipt email.
